01 Understanding AI today

A capable assistant, not a decision maker

Where it is strong

  • Drafts letters, briefs, slides and speaking notes from an outline
  • Summarises long reports, appeals and correspondence
  • Translates between Azerbaijani and other languages
  • Compares a document against a standard or best practice

Where judgement stays human

  • It can state something incorrect with full confidence
  • It has no authority and carries no accountability
  • Outputs must be checked against an authoritative source
  • Decisions of consequence remain with named officials
Capture the speed of language work — protect the judgement, verification and accountabilitySection 01
02 Three ways to use AI

Same model, three very different doors

Not for official work

Personal account

A private login on a public tool.

  • Prompts may be retained and used to train the model
  • No central control, no audit trail
  • Public, non-official information only
The starting point

Enterprise account

Contracted by the entity, centrally administered.

  • Does not train on your data
  • Central administration, access control, audit
  • Still processed on the vendor cloud
Most sensitive work

Fully secure, on-premise

A closed platform on state premises.

  • Data never leaves national control
  • Accredited for confidential and restricted data
  • Higher cost and effort to run
The door decides who sees your data, whether there is an audit trail, and which data may be used at allSection 02

07 Summary · where to start

A sequenced path to the secure environment

1
Weeks 1–8

Stand up the enterprise account

Contract it, fix data access before rollout, issue guidance on the three principles.

2
Quarter 1

Capture early value

Drafting, translation, summaries on public and internal data. Train widely.

3
Quarters 1–2

Accredit a secure room

A secure room in an existing state data centre — the fastest route to production.

4
Quarters 2–4

Prove high-value cases

A decision dashboard, case correspondence, cross-domain analysis on sensitive data.

5
Year 2+

Scale to sovereignty

Build the dedicated classified facility and wider stack as clearances grow.

Sequence, do not choose. For the secure environment, start with an accredited secure room on state premises — vendor-operated, faster to production — then grow into a fully state-owned dedicated facility as volumes and cleared staff grow.
