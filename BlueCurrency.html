Blue Currency  |  The problem we solve

Today's manual, disconnected workflows create real pain that our agentic AI removes

Module Key features How companies do it today Pain points Our solution
1AI Generated Unified BoQ
  • Unique code for every line item
  • Auto translate any format into one BoQ
 Cost items live as free text with no shared identity. Almost impossible to compare across projects, bidders or years. AI codes and maps every item into one standard automatically.
2Dynamic Bottom Up CAPEX Estimation
  • Item level forecasts from real cost drivers
  • Live commodity, labor and FX trends
 Estimates rely on simple inflation multipliers. Historical prices go stale and benchmarking stays manual. Bottom up forecasts stay current with live market data.
3Smart Tender Bid Evaluation
  • Bids mapped to one common structure
  • Outliers flagged and bids ranked
 Each bidder submits prices in their own format. Comparison takes weeks and scope gaps go unnoticed. Bids are normalized, scored and ranked in minutes.
4Agentic Procurement and Construction Progress Tracking
  • AI gatekeeper checks quantities and progress
  • One view from portfolio to line item
 Progress is reported through many manual channels. Overruns surface only once they are irreversible. AI flags mismatches early in plain language.
5Value Optimizer
  • Continuous value engineering
  • Savings mined from past patterns
 Value engineering happens in ad hoc workshops. Savings are missed or found far too late. An always on loop surfaces savings across the lifecycle.
★End to End Agentic Orchestration
  • One governed model across all stages
  • Every project makes the next one smarter
 Point tools each cover one isolated stage. No single model connects the full lifecycle. One agentic layer connects and learns across modules.
