Blue Currency  |  Competitive landscape

The market falls into four archetypes, and we sit deliberately between them

High AI-native reasoning Low
AI specialists
Machine learning products built for one step of the lifecycle, such as takeoff or site capture, that give a real AI answer inside that step only.
Example vendors
Kreo Buildots Costify Zebel ConWize Ediphi Doxel OpenSpace ALICE nPlan
Sharp on the one step they own, nothing carries across a handover.
Agentic platforms
One platform whose agents reason across every stage, carrying the BoQ, the estimate, the tender and site actuals forward as a single thread.
No one is here yet
Where the market is heading, and what Blue Currency is built for.
Point tools
Single-task software for measuring, pricing or marking up drawings, where every judgement is made by the operator rather than the tool.
Example vendors
Bluebeam Revu PlanSwift RIB CostX RIB Candy Sage Estimating Causeway Excel templates
Most of the market still sits here, no automation and no memory.
Enterprise suites
Systems of record that span many stages through configured modules and workflows, so breadth comes from setup rather than from reasoning.
Example vendors
Oracle Procore RIB iTWO Cleopatra SAP Ariba Autodesk ACC InEight Trimble Jaggaer
Broad but rules-based, more features and harder to use.
Blue Currency broader than the AI specialists, more AI-native than the enterprise suites
